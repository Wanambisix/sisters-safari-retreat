The Journey

Your Adventure Day by Day

Tap any day to see the full details and photos.

1

1st April — Nairobi

Upon arrival at the international Airport in Nairobi, you will be met by our representative for a short briefing and later drop off to your hotel in Nairobi. Spend the night at your hotel as you wait to prepare for your adventure the following morning.
2

2nd April — Lake Nakuru National Park

After breakfast, drive to Lake Nakuru National Park, a birdwatcher's paradise and a vital sanctuary for both black and white rhinos. Climb to the "Out of Africa" lookout point for a panoramic view of the lake and its shimmering birdlife. This National Park is also home to lions, leopard, buffaloes among many other wildlife. After the thrilling safari, head to the hotel for lunch and enjoy the afternoon at leisure. Dinner and overnight at the lodge.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
3

3rd April — Masai Mara National Reserve

After breakfast, check out and drive further along the Rift Valley. Journey to the world-renowned Masai Mara National Reserve. Arrive in time for a hotel buffet meal. Later head out for an introductory evening game drive across the open plains. Feel the excitement as the landscape transforms into the vast, open savannah teeming with wildebeests among many other wildlife. Return to the hotel lodge for dinner and overnight.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
4

4th April — The Ultimate Safari in Masai Mara

A full day with picnic lunch in the Great Masai Mara. Witness the high-stakes drama of the savannah and explore the rolling hills for the Big Five. Your 4x4 Land Cruiser will navigate the terrain to find the best sightings of predators on the hunt and witness the wildebeest migration along the Mara River among many dramatic wildlife encounters. Later on in the evening, you will experience a Masai village cultural visit in one of the nearby villages, understand how they are so brave to live so close to the wildlife.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
5

5th April — Lake Naivasha

After breakfast, drive to the shores of Lake Naivasha. Enjoy the serene atmosphere of this freshwater lake, famous for its hippo population and birdlife. Enjoy a relaxed boat ride on Lake Naivasha and an easy walking safari or an optional horse ride close to some friendly animals like giraffe, zebras, gazelles etc. All meals at the Lake Naivasha Country Club, often visited by zebras and waterbucks.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
6

6th April — Fly to Diani

After breakfast, begin your journey back to Nairobi, arriving in the city for your local flight to Diani. You will first have lunch in Nairobi then fly out into Southern Palms Diani. Check in to your beachfront resort and spend the afternoon at leisure with the beach and pools at your disposal.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
7

7th April — Diani — Dhow Cruise and Snorkeling

Wake up early for a sunrise dhow cruise with a light breakfast before heading over to Robinson Island for a snorkeling experience. There is an optional Jet Ski experience to be charged separately at Robinson Island for the more adventurous ones. Head back to the hotel for lunch with the afternoon at leisure. Dinner and overnight at the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
8

8th April — Kongo River

Spend the morning at leisure. After lunch head out for some adrenaline from ziplining over water view and later enjoy a relaxed sunset canoe ride in Kongo River. Head back to the hotel for dinner and overnight.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
9

9th April — Fly to Nairobi

Check out and head out to Ukunda Airstrip for your flight back to Nairobi. After check-in, we will go souvenir shopping as we say goodbye to what has been a carefully curated safari experience with an amazing farewell dinner.
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
10

10th April — Departure

Check out and be transferred to JKIA for your international flight.
Meals: Breakfast

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the food halal?

Yes. All meals are halal-certified. We work with lodges and restaurants that understand halal requirements. Dietary restrictions can be accommodated.

Is Kenya safe for tourists?

Yes. Kenya is one of Africa's leading tourism destinations. You will be accompanied by experienced local hosts and guides throughout the trip.

What's the group size?

Maximum 25 women. Most departures have 15–20 guests, ensuring a personal experience.

Can I bring my child?

For specific group safari, only girls 14 years and above are allowed.

What is included in the package?

Accommodation, domestic flights within Kenya, airport transfers, ground transportation, safari activities, park fees, meals as specified in the itinerary, and guided excursions.

What should I pack?

Light neutrals for game drives, swimwear for Diani, a light jacket for mornings, sunscreen, and camera. A detailed packing list is sent after booking.

Are there prayer facilities on the safari?

Absolutely. Prayer breaks are scheduled throughout each day, and our lodges provide quiet, clean spaces for Salah. The guide coordinates timings based on the daily prayer schedule.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. A $500 USD deposit secures your spot. The balance is then paid in 5 monthly instalments — $600/month for Twin/Double sharing (full $3,500) or $680/month for Single occupancy (full $3,900).

Is travel insurance required?

It's a requirement but recommendable at personal expense.
